# Privacy Policy for Drawing Pro

**Last Updated**: March 30, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how **Drawing Pro** ("we", "us", or "our"), published by **Swapnull**, handles your information when you use our mobile application **Drawing Pro** (package name: `com.swapnull.drawingpro`).

We respect your privacy. Drawing Pro is designed as a private, on-device digital sketchpad and drawing tool. **We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Drawing Pro does not collect any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII).**

- **No User Accounts**: You can use all features of Drawing Pro without creating an account or providing any personal information.
- **No Analytics or Tracking**: We do not use third-party analytics services, tracking tools, or advertising SDKs.
- **No Data Uploads**: All drawings, sketches, canvas edits, and imported photos remain 100% stored locally on your device.

---

## 2. Device Permissions Used

Drawing Pro requests the minimum necessary permissions to provide core drawing and tracing features:

### A. Camera Permission (`android.permission.CAMERA`)
- **Purpose**: Allows you to take a photo using your device camera to use as a background template for drawing or tracing.
- **Usage & Privacy**: Camera captures are processed exclusively on your device in memory. Camera images are never collected, stored remotely, or transmitted to any external server.

### B. Photos & Storage Access (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / MediaStore)
- **Purpose**: Allows you to select an image from your photo gallery as a drawing background and save your completed artwork to your device's `Pictures/` folder.
- **Usage & Privacy**: Your media files stay strictly local to your device. We do not access, view, or read any unselected photos.

---

## 3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Drawing Pro does **not** share any data with third parties.

- We do **not** sell, rent, or trade any user information.
- The app operates offline and does **not** transmit data to external servers.
- The app contains **no advertising** and no ad-network SDKs.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

Drawing Pro is suitable for users of all ages, including children under 13. Because the application does not collect any personal information from any user, it is fully compliant with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**.

---

## 5. Security

Because all artwork, photos, and settings remain solely on your local device, your data security is protected by Android's built-in sandbox security model.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
