Privacy Policy for Drawing Pro

Effective Date: March 30, 2025 | Package: com.swapnull.drawingpro

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Drawing Pro ("we", "us", or "our"), published by Swapnull, handles your information when you use our Android application.

Drawing Pro is designed as a private, on-device digital sketchpad and drawing tool. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Drawing Pro does not collect any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No User Accounts: You can use all features of Drawing Pro without creating an account or providing personal details.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics services, tracking tools, or advertising SDKs.
  • No Data Uploads: All drawings, sketches, canvas edits, and imported photos remain 100% stored locally on your device.

2. Device Permissions Used

Drawing Pro requests the minimum necessary permissions to provide core drawing features:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Allows you to take a photo using your device camera to use as a background template for drawing or tracing. Camera captures are processed exclusively on your device in memory and are never collected or transmitted.
  • Photos & Storage Access (MediaStore): Allows you to select an image from your gallery as a background and save completed artwork to your device's Pictures/ folder. Your files stay strictly local to your device.

3. Data Sharing & Advertising

Drawing Pro does not share any data with third parties. The app contains no advertising and operates offline without transmitting data to external servers.

4. Children’s Privacy

Drawing Pro is suitable for users of all ages, including children under 13. Because the application does not collect any personal information, it is fully compliant with COPPA and GDPR regulations.
